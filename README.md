## Deadzone
Deadzone is a 2D top-down horde survival shooter game where you play as a grizzled soldier fending for your life against unending waves of zombies. Acquire 4 distinct weapons across the game, and face off against an array of different enemies, from acid spitting to explosive time bombs mutants.
